About Meدرباره من

I'm KIavashmax. I write code, work on AI architectures, make games, play guitar, rap, and do 3D design. Right now, my main focus is developing SMC-Net and the NeuroCache models. I'm also part of the MAKA team, where I run our sub-team MaxLendGames. We're currently rebuilding the old survival game OXIDE under the new name ORKIDE.

AI & Architectureهوش مصنوعی و معماری‌ها

SMC-Net (Self-Modulating Cache Network) is the core architecture I'm building. The NeuroCache models are different versions of this architecture, tested for different trade-offs between speed, memory, and accuracy.

SMC-Net معماری اصلی هسته‌ایه که دارم روش کار می‌کنم. مدل‌های NeuroCache نسخه‌های مختلفی از این معماری هستن که برای تعادل بین سرعت، حافظه و دقت تست می‌شن.
